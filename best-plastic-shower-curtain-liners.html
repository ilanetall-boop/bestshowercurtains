Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best material for a plastic shower curtain liner?

PEVA and polyester blends are generally the better choice for the best plastic shower curtain liners because they resist mildew and water beading better than plain vinyl, and they do not carry the strong chemical smell some vinyl liners have when new. Every liner in this comparison other than the MitoVilla, which is PEVA-only, uses a polyester and PEVA blend.

What size shower curtain liner do I need?

Most US tubs and shower stalls use a standard 72 by 72 inch liner, which covers five of the seven products in this guide. If your stall is shorter than a standard tub, a 72 by 65 inch liner like the Biscaynebay Fabric Shower Curtain Short or the AmazerBath Short Shower Curtain Plastic sits closer to the floor without excess material pooling at the bottom.

How often should I replace a plastic shower curtain liner?

Most owners report replacing a plastic shower curtain liner every six to twelve months, depending on ventilation and how often it gets wiped down or run through a wash cycle. A liner that develops visible mildew spots or a sticky texture despite cleaning has reached the end of its useful life, regardless of how recently it was purchased.

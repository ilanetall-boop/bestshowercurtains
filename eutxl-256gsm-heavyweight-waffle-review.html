Frequently Asked Questions

What does 256GSM mean on a shower curtain?

GSM stands for grams per square meter, a standard measure of fabric weight. At 256GSM, the EUTXL Heavyweight Waffle is denser than the thin, sub-100GSM polyester curtains that cling to your skin and billow with the airflow from a shower. The higher weight is what lets a fabric curtain hang flat against a tub or shower stall without a separate liner doing all the work.

Does the EUTXL 256GSM Heavyweight Waffle need a liner?

The listing markets it as a standalone curtain made from a polyester and PEVA blend, so it's built to handle water exposure without a separate liner behind it. If your bathroom sees heavy daily use, pairing it with a liner still extends the life of any fabric curtain, waffle weave included.

What size is the EUTXL 256GSM Heavyweight Waffle Weave?

It measures 71 inches wide by 74 inches long and ships as a single pack. That's the standard footprint for most bathtubs and shower stalls in US homes, so it should fit without trimming or a custom rod. If you want a longer drop, the WOUVMA Extra Long option above runs bigger for taller stalls.

What Could Be Better

The single-size cut is the drawback that matters most for this BOODII no hook shower curtain. It ships only in 72 by 72 inches, so if your rod runs wider than a standard tub or your ceiling calls for an extra-long drop, BOODII doesn't offer a bigger variant and you would need an extra-wide or extra-long no-hook curtain from another brand instead. BOODII also sells the curtain on its own, with no rod or liner bundled in, so a bathroom that doesn't already have a rod needs a separate purchase, such as the BRIOFOX Tension Curtain Rod covered below. And while the Polyester and PEVA blend is practical, it won't give you the heavier, waffle-textured feel that RiverDream's fabric offers at nearly the same price. Reviewers consistently note that the fit works well for a standard tub, but anyone with a nonstandard shower stall should measure carefully before buying.

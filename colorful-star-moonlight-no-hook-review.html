The Colorful Star Moonlight Blue No curtain pairs a polyester shell with a PEVA liner layer, a combination meant to give you the water resistance of a plastic liner without the stiff, cling-prone feel that plain PEVA curtains are known for. At 72 inches wide and 75 inches long, it fits a standard tub or shower opening and comes as a single curtain rather than a liner-plus-curtain bundle, so you are not pairing it with a separate liner unless you want extra moisture protection.

The no-hook header is the feature doing most of the selling here. Rather than requiring a dozen rings, the curtain's top edge loops directly over the rod through built-in fabric channels, which cuts down on the clinking noise and ring replacement that comes with standard curtains. Verified buyers consistently note that the moon and star print stays vivid after repeated washes, though a handful mention a plastic odor straight out of the packaging that fades within a day or two of airing it out.

What We Liked

The biggest draw of this colorful star moonlight no-hook curtain is the hardware, or the lack of it. Skipping separate rings means one less thing to buy, lose, or replace when a cheap metal hook inevitably bends. Reviewers consistently note that the fabric hangs with a cleaner, more even drape than ring-hung curtains, since there is no uneven gap between each hook. The print itself earns praise too. Buyers describe the blue background and scattered stars as more saturated in person than typical product photos suggest, which helps it stand out in a market full of flat, single-tone shower curtains. At $23.99, it also undercuts several printed competitors that charge $30 or more for a similar look.

What Could Be Better

The plastic odor some owners describe when first opening the curtain is the most common complaint, and while it tends to fade after airing the curtain out, it is worth knowing before your first shower. The fixed 72 by 75 inch size is another limitation. Owners with oversized tubs or stall showers report needing to size up elsewhere, since Colorful Star does not sell this print in a wider cut. A smaller number of reviews mention the no-hook header feeling snug on thicker decorative rods, so measuring your rod diameter before ordering avoids a return.

Who Is It For?

This curtain suits anyone redecorating a bathroom around blue, navy, or celestial accents who wants to avoid buying a separate pack of curtain rings. It also fits shoppers on a tighter budget, since $23.99 keeps it below most patterned curtains with comparable print detail. It is a weaker fit for stall showers, clawfoot tubs, or any setup needing a nonstandard width, and buyers sensitive to new-plastic smells may want to let it air out in a garage or spare room before hanging it. If a no-hook design matters more than the specific star print, the Colorful Star No Hook Shower curtain covers similar ground at a slightly lower price, which we cover below.
